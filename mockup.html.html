Eco Enzym Production

A multi-purpose liquid produced from the fermentation of organic waste.

Floor Cleaner

Air Purifier

Ratio 1:1000. Mist into the air to remove odors and harmful bacteria.

Eco Brick Construction

Transforming non-recyclable plastic into durable building blocks.

01

Clean & Dry

Wash and dry all soft plastics to prevent bacterial growth inside the brick.

02

Cut Into Bits

Cut plastic into smaller pieces to allow for higher density and easier packing.

03

Pack Tight

Use a wooden stick to compress the plastic into a 500ml or 1.5L PET bottle.

04

Weight Check

Ensure the brick meets the minimum density (0.33g/ml) for construction safety.

Maggot BSF Lifecycle

egg

Egg Stage

Eggs are laid in wood crevices and hatch within 3-4 days into tiny larvae.

nutrition

Growth (Waste Consumption)

Larvae consume organic waste voraciously for 14-21 days, growing rapidly.

conversion_path

Prepupa & Pupa

Larvae stop eating and migrate to a dry area to begin their transformation.

flutter_dash

Adult BSF

The fly emerges, lives for 5-8 days without eating, only to mate and lay eggs.

Processing Capacity

2-5 kg/day
